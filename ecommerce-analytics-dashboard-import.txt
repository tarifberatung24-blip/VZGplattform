2.5 Charts and data visualization
- Use Recharts or VZG equivalent chart system; never replace charts with static screenshots.
- Bar chart: paired Sales and Revenue bars, rounded top corners, gray/orange series; May is emphasized in the dashboard profit chart.
- Area chart: indigo line/fill gradient for customer orders.

- Donut charts: inner radius 50, outer radius 70-80, 2px padding between slices.
- Chart grid: light dashed horizontal grid, no vertical grid where possible; no visible axis lines; compact 11-12px ticks.
- Tooltips: rounded 8px surface with border/shadow, theme-aware colors, formatted currency/counts.
- Preserve legends, labels and accessible chart descriptions.
- Source dashboard includes a country revenue donut rather than a geographic map; retain the card title "Sales by Countries" and the country legend.

2.6 Responsive breakpoints
- Mobile base: under 640px. Stack all grids, hide desktop-only header nav and page-header actions when space is constrained, allow horizontal scrolling for pills/tables.
- Small: 640px and above. Two-column metric grids and visible page-header actions.
- Large: 1024px and above. Three/four-column dashboard grids and full header navigation.
- Extra-wide: preserve max-width 1400px and centered alignment.
- Ensure chart heights remain usable: dashboard profit 200px, customer orders 120px, sales chart 300px, reports chart 250px, donut approximately 180-200px.
