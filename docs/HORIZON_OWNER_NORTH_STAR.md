# HORIZON Owner North Star

**Owner direction recorded:** 2026-10-18  
**Status:** long-term product direction — not a statement of current capability, affiliation, licence, or integration.

## The purpose

HORIZON by VZG is being built to give people in Germany a clearer path through everyday financial,
contractual, and administrative matters. The product begins with German and Bulgarian support so that
users can understand their situation, prepare the right information, and retain control of every
important decision.

The long-term aim is not to create another form portal. It is to build a trusted operating layer that
reduces avoidable complexity for users and, through approved partnerships, helps institutions and
providers receive better prepared, traceable requests.

The four names remain distinct: **Tarifberater24** is the legal entity, **VZG CONSULT** is the expert
brand, **HORIZON by VZG** is the customer product, and **VZGplattform** is the codebase.

## The destination

### 1. Contract control and professional support

Users should eventually be able to manage and understand all relevant tariff and recurring-cost
contracts through HORIZON: energy, insurance, banking and other approved product areas. The target
experience includes a clear view of provider, price, term, notice period, documents, status, and
next action; secure links into a participating provider's customer portal where that partner permits
it; and access to professional customer support for complex cases.

HORIZON aims for direct partnerships with leading energy, insurance and banking institutions in
Germany. A partnership must improve the user's real outcome, be transparent about compensation,
and be implemented only with the partner's approved technical and legal process.

### 2. Digital bridge to consumer, public and social institutions

HORIZON should become a practical support tool for consumer-protection organisations and public or
social institutions. The intended value is to help their users arrive with a better structured case:
documents collected, facts confirmed, the situation explained in the user's language, and the
official communication prepared in high-standard German.

The future target is approved, secure, digital communication with participating institutions. A
workflow may prepare requests, applications, documents, data, objections and follow-up questions in
the institution's required format. It may transmit them only when a lawful, approved integration
exists and the user has given the required approval.

HORIZON never replaces the authority's expert or legal decision. The institution retains final
review and decision authority. Where an institutional partnership permits it, the desired outcome is
a case packet so complete and structured that an authorised expert needs only to review it and make
the final decision.

### 3. HORIZON Capital

After the control and optimisation layers are mature, HORIZON Capital is intended to complete the
user's financial picture. Users should be able to build a financial portfolio view, follow income and
expenses, define personal goals, and receive understandable financial analysis and a plan for short-
and long-term progress.

Any future investment analysis, recommendation, execution, custody, or partner connection is
conditional on the necessary legal permissions, regulated partners, suitability and risk processes,
user approvals, and applicable consumer-protection requirements. HORIZON will not present these
future capabilities as available before those conditions are met.

## Operating promise

Every new layer follows the same sequence:

```text
understand → analyse → explain → user reviews → user approves → authorised party executes → status returns
```

The product must remain evidence-led, multilingual, accessible, privacy-conscious, and clear about
what HORIZON can and cannot do. Automation may reduce repetitive work, but it must not hide a
decision, manufacture an entitlement, invent a saving, or substitute an authorised human decision.

## Partnership standard

HORIZON seeks relationships that are more valuable than a referral link. The preferred model is a
closed but user-controlled workflow: HORIZON prepares a consented, high-quality case; the partner
performs its regulated or institutional process; and only the necessary status returns to HORIZON so
the user can follow progress. Each partnership needs explicit agreements on data roles, permitted
data, consent, security, auditability, support responsibilities, compensation, and cancellation or
error handling.

## What this document does not authorise

This North Star does not authorise database changes, external integrations, institution access,
provider portal access, submission of user data, electronic signing, investment activity, regulated
financial advice, or automatic decisions. Each must be separately researched, approved, implemented,
tested, and governed under the Master Map and project rules.

## Founder statement

The product began with limited resources and a commitment to solve real problems for people who are
often overwhelmed by contracts, documents, costs and institutions. The standard for every future
decision is simple: does it give the user a clearer, safer and more useful HORIZON?
