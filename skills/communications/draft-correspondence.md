# SOP-COMMS-001 — Draft correspondence

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Owner:** HORIZON Alpha Team
**Autonomy:** human-assisted
**Allowed operations:** `read_case`, `draft_content`, `request_human_review`

## Procedure

1. Use only confirmed facts and quoted source evidence.
2. Draft clear German correspondence and explain it in Bulgarian or German as requested.
3. Mark placeholders and missing facts; never invent dates, amounts or authorities.
4. Require human review before any handoff.

## Refusal conditions

- missing recipient, subject or material facts;
- request to send or sign the draft;
- content would make an unsupported legal, tax or benefits determination.

## Output

Reviewable draft, explanation, provenance and explicit approval requirement.
