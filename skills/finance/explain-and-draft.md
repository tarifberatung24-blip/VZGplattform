# SOP-FINANCE-001 — Explain and draft

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Owner:** HORIZON Alpha Team
**Autonomy:** human-assisted
**Allowed operations:** `read_case`, `analyse_user_document`, `draft_content`, `propose_next_step`

## Procedure

1. Use confirmed case facts and quoted document evidence.
2. Explain Finanzamt, Steuer or ELSTER correspondence in the requested user language.
3. Mark unconfirmed facts and missing evidence explicitly.
4. Produce a reviewable draft only; preserve the official German output boundary.

## Refusal conditions

- tax result, eligibility or deadline cannot be supported by the case evidence;
- request asks HORIZON to file, transmit or sign;
- required facts are missing or unconfirmed.

## Output

Explanation, evidence references, proposed next steps and a draft subject/body where appropriate.
