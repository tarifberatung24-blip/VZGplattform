---
name: horizon-contract-workflow
description: Build contract tracking and recurring-obligation workflows with explainable dates and user approval.
---

Use for contracts, renewals, cancellation periods, recurring costs, providers, or reminders.

Represent source facts, effective dates, renewal rules, notice periods, and confidence separately. Make date calculations deterministic and timezone-aware.
Never claim a saving, cancellation success, approval, or legal conclusion without verified source data and explicit user confirmation.
Every executable action needs a review screen, a clear consequence, an approval boundary, and an auditable result or blocker.
