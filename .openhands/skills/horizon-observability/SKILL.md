---
name: horizon-observability
description: Add safe, useful telemetry for agent runs, tools, latency, errors, and user-approved actions.
---

Use for debugging orchestration, connectors, workflows, or production-like smoke tests.

Record correlation ID, operation, duration, outcome category, retry count, and tool/model identifiers. Never log secrets, raw prompts with customer data, full documents, or authentication headers. Make traces useful without making them a second customer-data store.
