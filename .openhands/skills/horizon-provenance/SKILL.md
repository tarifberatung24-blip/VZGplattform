---
name: horizon-provenance
description: Add and review source-backed evidence for calculations, rules, recommendations, and extracted facts.
---

Use when a feature displays a fact, deadline, recommendation, score, calculation, or partner option.

For each material result, identify source, source date, input values, transformation/rule version, confidence, and freshness. Keep unknown values unknown.
Separate deterministic calculations from model-generated explanations. Display uncertainty and missing inputs instead of filling gaps.
Prefer immutable evidence references and append-only audit events; do not store secrets or raw customer data in logs.
