---
name: horizon-render-ops
description: Diagnose Render builds, deploys, logs, and runtime health without changing production configuration implicitly.
---

Use for Render deployment or runtime incidents.

Separate source/build failure, environment failure, runtime failure, and route failure. Read logs with secrets redacted, identify the exact commit and service, and reproduce locally where possible. Do not redeploy, change env vars, domains, scaling, or service settings without owner approval. Verify the actual deployed route after an authorized deploy.
