description: Budget multi-model and connector work with quotas, deadlines, caching, and graceful degradation.

Use for parallel models, expensive research, OCR, browser tasks, or long-running workflows.

Set per-task token, time, model-count, and connector-call budgets. Cache safe read-only results with freshness metadata. Cancel slow or duplicate workers and return partial results with an explicit confidence downgrade. Never trade away auth, provenance, or approval boundaries to meet a deadline.
