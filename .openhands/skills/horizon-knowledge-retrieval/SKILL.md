Use for library APIs, regulations, provider capabilities, product rules, or external documentation.

Prefer primary and current sources. Record source URL, version/date, query intent, and uncertainty. Do not convert search snippets into facts. Separate retrieved facts from model inference and mark information requiring owner or legal review.
