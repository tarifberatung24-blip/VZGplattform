---
name: horizon-i18n-quality
description: Verify German, Bulgarian, and English localization without hardcoded customer-facing strings.
---

Use for UI copy, messages, routes, labels, errors, emails, or locale changes.

- Put user-facing strings in the established message catalogs and preserve interpolation variables.
- Run the repository i18n checks and inspect missing, unused, and hardcoded strings.
- Keep legal, consent, affiliate, privacy, and regulated wording semantically exact across locales.
- Do not translate identifiers, route paths, environment names, database names, URLs, or partner IDs.
- Check pluralization, date/number formatting, text expansion, and empty/error states in all supported locales.
