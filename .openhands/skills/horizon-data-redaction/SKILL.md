---
name: horizon-data-redaction
description: Remove secrets and sensitive customer data from prompts, logs, traces, screenshots, and model handoffs.
---

Use before sending data to a model, connector, plugin, browser, or observability system.

Classify data, minimize fields, redact tokens and identifiers, and use synthetic examples for tests. Do not paste `.env`, service-role keys, private URLs, customer documents, or full database rows into an agent context. Verify redaction before transmission and report what was intentionally omitted.
