---
name: horizon-task-decomposition
description: Convert a broad platform request into small phase-approved deliverables with measurable acceptance criteria.
---

Use for roadmap, “finish everything”, or cross-cutting requests.

Separate discovery, design, implementation, verification, and handoff. Map each item to an approved phase and allowed files. Define blockers and the smallest demonstrable result. Do not inflate a checklist with unimplemented placeholders or mark a phase complete because documentation exists.
